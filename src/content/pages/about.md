## What Is This Place?

**FuckedCompany.com** is the spiritual successor to Philip Kaplan's legendary FuckedCompany.com (2000-2007)—the site that tracked dot-com busts, layoffs, and corporate implosions with gleeful schadenfreude. When the first bubble burst, F*ckedCompany was there. When startups went from "disrupting" to "disrupted," F*ckedCompany was there. And now? Now we're back. Because there's a new bubble. And it's powered by AI.

We track the same chaos, but the script has flipped. This time it's not Pets.com running out of money. It's companies running out of *humans*. AI-driven layoffs. Automation announcements. Job displacement dressed up as "efficiency" and "strategic transformation." We're here to document it all with the same dark humor and unflinching honesty that made the original great.

## What We Track

- **AI-driven layoffs** — When companies announce they're "going AI-first" and half the workforce gets a calendar invite from HR
- **Automation rollouts** — When your job gets "augmented" (read: replaced) by an agent
- **The dead pool** — Companies, roles, and entire industries we're betting will get disrupted next
- **The cope** — Angry rants from people whose jobs were taken by worse versions of themselves
- **Retraining opportunities** — Satirical job postings for the new economy (Chief Vibes Officer, anyone?)

## How the Dead Pool Works

Think of it as fantasy football for the apocalypse. You pick which companies, roles, or trends will get "disrupted" (read: destroyed) by AI. You earn points based on accuracy and rarity. The more people who picked the same thing, the fewer points you get. The more obscure your correct prediction, the bigger the payoff. It's morbid. It's addictive. It's probably the only accurate forecasting model we have left.

### Point System

| Outcome | Base Points |
|---------|-------------|
| **AUTOMATED** | 100 pts — The role/company was fully replaced by AI |
| **DOWNSIZED** | 50 pts — Significant layoffs attributed to AI/automation |
| **ON_NOTICE** | 25 pts — Official announcements of AI adoption targeting this area |
| **RUMOR** | 10 pts — Unconfirmed but plausible reports |

**Rarity multiplier:** If you were one of the few to predict something that came true, your points get multiplied. If everyone picked it, you get the base. We reward contrarian accuracy. Or maybe we're just rewarding doom-mongering. Same thing, really.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this site serious or a joke?**  
A: Yes. We're deadly serious about documenting real economic displacement. We're also incapable of being serious about anything. The tone is the point.

**Q: Who runs the leaderboard?**  
A: The leaderboard is maintained by **the prophecy council**—a shadowy cabal of unemployed data scientists and bitter ex-engineers who have nothing left to lose. They may or may not exist. The points are real.

**Q: Can I submit a company or role to the dead pool?**  
A: Absolutely. We welcome all predictions. Just know that if you're right, you'll feel terrible. If you're wrong, you'll feel... also terrible, but for different reasons.

**Q: What happened to the original FuckedCompany.com?**  
A: Philip Kaplan shut it down in 2007. The domain changed hands. The spirit never died. We're carrying the torch—or the Molotov cocktail, depending on your perspective.
