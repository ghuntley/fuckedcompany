I used to care about quality. I used to REVIEW things. I used to say "this isn't ready to ship" and actually mean it. Now? The AI produces slop. Absolute slop. And it gets shipped. Every. Single. Time. Because it's CHEAP. Because it's FAST. Because nobody in leadership has to look at the actual output. They just see the metrics. Lines of code? UP. Tickets closed? UP. Cost per feature? DOWN. Quality? QUALITY ISN'T A KPI ANYMORE.

Last week I watched the AI "write" a customer-facing email. It had three grammatical errors, recommended a product we discontinued in 2024, and addressed the customer as "Dear Valued Meatbag." MEATBAG. It went out to 50,000 people. Fifty thousand. My old manager would have had an aneurysm. The new one? "We'll A/B test a fix in Q3." Q3. We're in January. The damage is done. The damage is ALWAYS done.

I'm not even mad at the AI. The AI is a tool. It does what it's told. I'm mad at the people who decided that "good enough" is the new standard. Who decided that shipping broken garbage is fine as long as the quarterly numbers look nice. Who decided that the humans who actually gave a shit were "redundant." I'm mad at the whole system. And I'm mad that I'm sitting here watching it happen, unemployed, while the slop keeps flowing.

Quality is dead. I'm just here to watch the funeral.
