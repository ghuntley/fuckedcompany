The all-hands was Tuesday. He stood there in his casual Friday sweater (it was Tuesday, but you know, he's the CEO) and talked about "AI-first transformation." About how we were "leading the industry." About "exciting changes" and "strategic realignment." He used the word "synergy" three times. SYNERGY. He said we were "empowering our teams with cutting-edge AI tools to drive innovation." He got a standing ovation. People were PUMPED. I was in the back, already suspicious. I've been through enough reorgs to smell the blood.

Thursday. Half the company got a calendar invite. "HR Discussion - Please join." We all knew. We all knew the moment we saw it. "AI-first" meant "humans-last." "Strategic realignment" meant "you're aligned right out the door." "Empowering our teams" meant "empowering our teams to clean out their desks." Five hundred people. Gone. In a single day. The AI tools we were supposedly "empowering" ourselves with? Those were our replacements. Not our tools. Our REPLACEMENTS.

The severance package included "career transition support" and "access to our AI-powered resume optimizer." I'm not making that up. They fired us and then offered us an AI tool to help us find new jobs. Jobs that probably don't exist anymore because every company is doing the same thing. The lack of self-awareness is staggering. Or maybe it's perfect awareness. Maybe they know exactly what they're doing and they just don't care. Either way, I'm done. I'm so done with all of it.

If your CEO says "AI-first" in an all-hands, start updating your resume. That's not a strategy. That's a eulogy.
