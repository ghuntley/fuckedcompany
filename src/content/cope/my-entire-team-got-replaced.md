Eight people. EIGHT. We had a designer, two frontend devs, a backend dev, a QA engineer, a PM, and a DevOps person. We shipped features. We had standups. We argued about whether the button should be blue or slightly-more-blue. We were a TEAM. Last Monday they called us all into a Zoom. "We're going in a new direction." One AI agent. ONE. It's supposed to do everything we did. Design, code, test, deploy, project manage. All of it.

Let me tell you what this "agent" actually does. It generates UI mockups that look like they were designed in 2012. It writes code that breaks in production. It "tests" by running the same happy path three times. It has no idea what our users actually need. But here's the thing—it costs 99% less than us. NINETY-NINE PERCENT. So from a spreadsheet perspective? We never stood a chance.

I was the last one to go. They kept me for "knowledge transfer." I spent two weeks documenting everything for the AI. Our architecture. Our deployment process. Our tribal knowledge. I literally trained my replacement. And my replacement is a glorified chatbot that hallucinates database schemas. The irony is so thick you could spread it on toast.

Now I'm unemployed and the product is on fire. But the stock price went up 4% when they announced the "efficiency gains." So I guess it's fine. Everything is fine.
