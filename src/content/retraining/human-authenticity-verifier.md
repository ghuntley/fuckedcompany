**TrustNoBot Inc.** is seeking **Human Authenticity Verifiers** to ensure that content, code, and communication was actually produced by humans. Yes, we see the irony. No, we're not hiring an AI to do this. Yet.

## About the Role

In a world where AI generates everything—emails, code, articles, love letters—someone has to verify what's "real." That someone is you. You will review outputs and determine: human or machine? The criteria are vague. The training is minimal. The existential weight of deciding what counts as "authentic human expression" is entirely on you. Good luck!

## Responsibilities

- Review content (text, code, images) and verify human authorship
- Develop "gut feelings" about AI vs. human output (we'll call this "expertise")
- Click through CAPTCHAs to prove you're human (meta-verification)
- Document patterns that distinguish human work (for the AI that will eventually replace you)
- Maintain hope that your job has meaning

## Requirements

- Must be verifiably human (we'll send you a CAPTCHA)
- Keen eye for the subtle tells of AI generation (overly polite, no typos, suspiciously coherent)
- Comfort with the knowledge that you're training the system that will obsolete you
- Ability to work from anywhere (we need to verify your location is real too)

## Compensation

- $28/hour (until we automate this)
- Generous existential dread package
- First in line to be replaced by our "Human Detection AI" (currently in development)

*TrustNoBot Inc.: Proving you're human, one verification at a time. Until we don't need to.*
