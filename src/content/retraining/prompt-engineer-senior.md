**MegaCorp** is seeking a **Senior Prompt Engineer (L7)** to join our AI-Powered Engineering division.

## About the Role

Remember when you were a Senior Software Engineer? Those were the days. $350K total comp, RSUs, the respect of your peers. Well, we have a NEW opportunity for you! Same "Senior" title. Same "L7" level. Completely different job. You'll be writing prompts. Lots of them. Exciting, right?

## Responsibilities

- Craft sophisticated prompts to elicit quality outputs from our internal AI coding assistant
- Iterate on prompt templates (add "please," try "thank you," experiment with "think step by step")
- Review AI-generated code and provide feedback in the form of... better prompts
- Attend daily standups where you explain what prompts you wrote yesterday
- Maintain a straight face when someone calls this "engineering"

## Requirements

- 5+ years of software engineering experience (we'll pay you like you have 0)
- Ability to type "please" and "thank you" to a language model with genuine sincerity
- Familiarity with prompt engineering best practices (or ability to Google them)
- Willingness to accept 87% pay cut as "career transition"
- Strong communication skills (you'll be communicating with machines all day)

## Compensation

- $45,000/year (we're being generous—some companies offer $35K)
- "Growth opportunity" to become Principal Prompt Engineer (L8) at $52,000
- Stock options in the company that replaced you with AI
