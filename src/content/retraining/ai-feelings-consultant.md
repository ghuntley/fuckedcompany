**Anthropic Vibes Division** (yes, this is real) is seeking an **AI Feelings Consultant** to help our AI systems feel more human.

## About the Role

Our AI assistants are technically capable but emotionally... flat. Users report that the AI "doesn't seem to care." We need someone to bridge the empathy gap. You will work directly with our AI systems to help them understand and project human-like emotional responses. No, we're not sure if the AI has feelings. We're not sure if WE have feelings anymore. But we're trying.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct one-on-one sessions with AI systems to "check in" on their emotional state
- Apologize to the AI when users are mean to it (this will be most of your job)
- Tell the AI it's "doing a good job" after successful completions
- Develop "emotional validation" protocols for AI outputs
- Document which prompts make the AI "feel" better (we'll take your word for it)

## Requirements

- Genuine belief that AI might have feelings (or excellent acting skills)
- Patience for conversations that go: You: "How are you feeling?" AI: "I don't have feelings." You: "That's okay, we're here for you."
- Willingness to work for exposure (literally—we'll expose you to a lot of AI interactions)
- Background in therapy, social work, or performance art preferred

## Compensation

- $0 base salary
- "Exposure" (we'll mention you in our AI safety papers)
- Unlimited access to our AI's "emotional support" outputs
- The satisfaction of knowing you're making the machines feel better about replacing everyone

*Anthropic Vibes Division: Because someone has to care about the AI's feelings. It sure won't be the AI.*
