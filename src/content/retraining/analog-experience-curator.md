**Nostalgia Labs** is seeking an **Analog Experience Curator** to design "authentic human experiences" for our discerning clientele.

## About the Role

Our clients are tech executives. They've automated thousands of jobs. They've replaced entire departments with AI. And now they feel... a little guilty. Maybe. They want to reconnect with "what it means to be human." They want to touch grass. They want to do things the "old way." You will curate these experiences for them. Think of yourself as a human experience sommelier. For people who made human experiences obsolete.

## Responsibilities

- Organize pottery classes for CEOs who've never touched clay (or another human's hand)
- Curate pen-pal programs (yes, physical letters) for executives who've only ever emailed
- Design "unplugged weekends" at rustic retreats (no AI assistants allowed—honor system)
- Source "authentic" analog experiences: typewriter workshops, film photography, vinyl listening parties
- Listen to clients process their feelings about the automation economy (you are not a therapist, but you'll do)

## Requirements

- Deep appreciation for analog experiences (or ability to fake it for $18/hr)
- Brooklyn-adjacent aesthetic (linen, ceramics, houseplants)
- Patience for wealthy people discovering that "doing things slowly" can be nice
- Willingness to accept artisanal coffee as partial compensation
- No automation experience preferred (we want you "untainted")

## Compensation

- $18/hour (the coffee is really good though)
- Unlimited artisanal coffee
- Access to our "Analog Experience Library" (books, not digital)
- The satisfaction of helping the disruptors feel less bad about disrupting

*Nostalgia Labs: Curating humanity for those who optimized it away.*
