Five thousand bookkeepers. The people who kept the books. The debits. The credits. The "where did this receipt go." GONE. Automated accounting platforms connected to bank feeds. AI categorizes. AI reconciles. AI generates reports. Humans? Optional.

QuickBooks. Xero. The whole category went full automation. Link your accounts. The software does the rest. Small businesses used to need a bookkeeper. Now they need a subscription. The math is brutal. For the bookkeepers, anyway.

SURPRISE! Your job is now a prompt. "Categorize these transactions." "Generate the P&L." "Find the discrepancy." Done. CONGRATS on being replaced by a statistical parrot. The parrot might misclassify the occasional expense. The parrot might miss the fraud. But it's cheap. So cheap.

August 2025. The great bookkeeper purge. Five thousand people who understood spreadsheets and tax codes. Replaced by software that does both. The machines are coming for the bean counters. The beans have been counted. You lost.
