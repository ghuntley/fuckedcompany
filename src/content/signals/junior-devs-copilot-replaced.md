The career ladder just lost its bottom rungs. Junior dev roles? VANISHING. Why hire a greenhorn when Copilot can write the boilerplate? Why mentor when the AI can generate the PR description? The pipeline is broken.

Senior devs love it. They get to "focus on architecture." Translation: they prompt the AI and review the output. The juniors who would have learned from them? Nowhere to be found. The industry is eating its young.

SURPRISE! Your job is now a prompt. "Build me a REST API." Done. "Fix this bug." Done. CONGRATS on being replaced by a statistical parrot. The parrot ships code. The parrot doesn't need a junior salary. The parrot doesn't need to "grow."

No official body count. Just the quiet death of job postings. "3+ years experience required" used to be a filter. Now it's a survival requirement. Good luck getting those three years when nobody's hiring for year zero.
