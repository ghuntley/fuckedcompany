Two thousand people. Managers. Dev relations. The folks who made Google seem human. GONE. The flattening is here. And it's flattening careers into paste.

Turns out you don't need seven layers of management when AI can generate status reports. You don't need dev advocates when the documentation writes itself. You don't need anyone, really. Just engineers and the machines that might replace them next.

SURPRISE! Your job is now a prompt. The AI will run the standups. The AI will write the blog posts. The AI will smile at developers at conferences. CONGRATS on being replaced by a statistical parrot. At least the parrot doesn't need a 401k.

"Don't be evil" got retired. So did "we care about our people." The new motto: "Efficiency at all costs." Welcome to the new Google. Same as the old Google. Just with fewer humans to pretend otherwise.
