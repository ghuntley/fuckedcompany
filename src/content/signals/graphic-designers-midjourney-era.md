Three thousand designers. The people who made things look good. Replaced by "a woman with blue hair, corporate style, stock photo." Midjourney and DALL-E ate the bottom of the creative ladder. The agencies noticed. They did the math.

Why pay a junior designer $60K when the art director can prompt for 20 bucks a month? The client can't tell the difference. Half the time the AI output has six fingers. The client still approves it. Standards are dead.

SURPRISE! Your job is now a prompt. "Make it pop." "More vibrant." "Corporate but fun." CONGRATS on being replaced by a statistical parrot that generates pretty pictures without understanding composition, brand, or taste.

The creative class is shrinking. The "prompt engineer" class is a myth. The real winners are the clients who get mediocre work for pennies. The designers? They're learning to prompt. Or they're learning to code. Or they're learning to cope.
